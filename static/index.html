Core Capabilities

Intelligent Analysis, Automatically

Every complaint is processed instantly — no manual tagging required.

🔍

Auto-Categorization

Classifies each complaint into Delivery, Refund, Product Quality, or Customer Service automatically — no manual tagging required.

💬

Sentiment Analysis

Detects emotional tone — positive, neutral, or negative — using a lexicon-based NLP pipeline tuned for customer language.

🎯

Priority Scoring

Scores 0–100 based on sentiment, urgency keywords, category weight, and cluster size — so the team always knows what to tackle first.

🔗

Smart Clustering

Groups similar complaints using TF-IDF + K-Means to surface systemic issues before they escalate into larger problems.

🚨

Early Warnings

Alerts management when complaint volume spikes or high-priority clusters form, enabling proactive rather than reactive response.

📊

Analytics Dashboard

Real-time dashboards, trend charts, and AI-explained priority breakdowns give managers full visibility across all active complaints.

Workflow

How It Works

From submission to resolution — tracked every step of the way.

1

Customer Submits

A customer signs in and submits a complaint through the portal. The system assigns a unique complaint ID instantly.

2

AI Analyses

The AI pipeline immediately categorizes the complaint, scores sentiment & priority, and clusters it with related issues.

3

Management Resolves

Management investigates, updates the status, and sends a customer-visible response. The customer sees the resolution in real time.
